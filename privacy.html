Imprint Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Imprint is built so that what you record and write stays yours. We (the developer) do not collect, see, sell or share your personal information.

What stays on your devices and in your iCloud

  • Your content. Recordings, transcripts, notes, to-dos, photos, PDFs and saved links are stored on your devices and synced through your own private iCloud account (iCloud Drive and Apple's CloudKit private database). We have no access to your iCloud data.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Imprint has no sign-up, shows no ads, and includes no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code.

On-device processing

  • Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio is not uploaded to transcribe it.
  • Summaries, to-dos, Ask Imprint, recaps and suggestions use Apple Intelligence on your device by default. Your content isn't sent to us or to any AI service we run. If you choose to use your own AI (below), some of these can use it instead.

Permissions

Imprint asks only when a feature needs it, and you can change any of these in iOS Settings:

  • Microphone, to record.
  • Speech Recognition, to transcribe on your device.
  • Camera and Photos, to add pictures you choose.
  • Calendars, to show your events next to recordings and name a recording after a meeting. Imprint never changes your calendars.
  • Reminders, only if you choose to send to-dos to Apple Reminders.
  • Notifications, for reminders and the daily recap. These are scheduled on your device.

When Imprint uses the internet

  • iCloud sync, handled by Apple.
  • Links you save. When you save a web link, Imprint fetches that page (and, for some video and audio sites, their public preview service) to show its title, image and description. Those sites receive a normal web request, as if you had opened the link.
  • Purchases. Imprint Pro is sold through Apple. Payment is handled entirely by Apple, and we never see your payment details.
  • Your own AI (optional, off by default). If you add your own Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT) API key and turn it on, Imprint sends the text needed for a summary, an answer or a document (transcripts and the matching parts of your notes, never audio) directly from your iPhone to that company, using your key. In Open chat, Imprint sends what you type, and parts of your library only after you allow it each time. Spaces you keep on your iPhone are never sent. We never receive this data; the provider's terms for your account apply. Your key is stored in your iPhone's Keychain. To stop, turn it off or remove the key in Settings > AI & Privacy.

Files and other apps

If you turn on Notes & To-Dos as Files, your notes are also saved as Markdown files in your iCloud Drive's Imprint folder, where you (and apps you choose) can open them.

Deleting your data

Deleting something in Imprint removes it from your devices and your iCloud. To remove everything, delete the app and then remove Imprint's data in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children

We (the developer) collect no data from anyone, including children.

Beta testing

If you test Imprint through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in TestFlight's own privacy terms.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
