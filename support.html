Common questions

Where are my recordings stored? On your devices and in your private iCloud. Audio and photos also appear in iCloud Drive > Imprint.

Recordings are missing after reinstalling. Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account, then go to Settings > iCloud & Storage > Recover from iCloud Files.

How do I restore Imprint Pro on a new device? Open Imprint Pro in Settings and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription? In iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.
